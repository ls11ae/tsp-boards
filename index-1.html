Algorithms to play with

Browser games on algorithms and geometry from Chair 11 Algorithm Engineering, Department of Computer Science, TU Dortmund University.

Ruler of the Plane

Hands-on computational geometry in a medieval kingdom:

  • Light the Dungeon: how few torches will do? (art gallery problem)
  • Conquer the Land: build castles against the computer (Voronoi game)
  • Walk the Dog: how short can the leash be? (Fréchet distance)
  • Mow the Lawn: two mowers, short tours (lawn mowing)
  • Road Works: every village, little road (minimum spanning tree)

TSP Boards

Find the shortest round trip: the travelling salesperson problem, on maps of Germany, Europe and the world, with a globe you can turn. Every puzzle has been solved exactly, so you can see how close you are.

We also build the puzzles as wooden boards with nails and a string: if the end of the string reaches the zero mark on the scale, you have found the shortest tour.
